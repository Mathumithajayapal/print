Design & Quality Guidelines

What file formats do you accept for custom designs?

We accept high-resolution PNG and JPEG files. For apparel, we highly recommend transparent PNG files to ensure there are no unwanted white background borders around your design.

What is the recommended DPI for print files?

For the best print quality, we require your design files to be at least 300 DPI (dots per inch) created at the actual physical print size.

Do you print using CMYK or RGB color profiles?

Our printers utilize the CMYK color space. While you can upload RGB files, they will be automatically converted to CMYK before printing, which may cause slight color shifts. We recommend converting files to CMYK before uploading.

Can I print on the sleeves of the t-shirts?

Yes, sleeve printing is available on select premium garments, including heavy hoodies and our signature long-sleeve tees. Additional print location fees apply.

How do I avoid white backgrounds around my PNG designs?

Ensure that you export your PNG with transparency enabled from your design software (like Photoshop, Illustrator, or Canva). If there is a solid white background in your file, the printer will print a white box on the garment.

Do you offer embroidery in addition to DTG printing?

Yes, we offer premium embroidery services for hats, beanies, polo shirts, and select outerwear. All embroidery files undergo a one-time digitization process before production.

Billing & Account Management

When am I charged for an order?

You are only charged for the product and shipping costs when the order is successfully imported from your store and enters the production queue. The customer pays you first, and then we bill your payment method on file.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), as well as PayPal. You can also top up your PrintOn Wallet for faster checkout.

How do I change my default billing currency?

You can change your default billing and display currency at any time by navigating to Account Settings > Billing > Currency Preferences.

Can I set up multiple stores under one account?

Yes! Even on our standard free tier, you can link up to 5 different e-commerce storefronts under a single PrintOn account for streamlined management.

Are there volume discounts for large orders?

Yes. If you are placing a manual bulk order (25+ items), tiered discounts will automatically apply at checkout. For consistent high-volume monthly sales, consider upgrading to PrintOn Premium for blanket discounts.

How do I delete or pause my PrintOn account?

To permanently delete your account and all associated data, visit Account Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. To temporarily pause integration, you can disable the API link directly in your store dashboard.
